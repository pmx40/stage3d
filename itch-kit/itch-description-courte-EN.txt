Stage3D is a free, in-browser tool to plan your stages and events to real scale before you get on site.

Place consoles, speakers, racks, backline, musicians and lights in 2D and 3D, draw your cables, check delays, number your monitors, patch audio and DMX lights, plan a band changeover, and export a PDF tech sheet. Everything is modelled in metres with recognisable equipment (about 125 objects).

Designed by a sound engineer, for sound engineers and technicians. Made by PaulMX, built with the help of AI.

- Free, no account, no server: your plan stays in your browser and can be saved as a file.
- 5 languages: English, Français, Español, Deutsch, Italiano.
- A Windows .exe is also available to download.
- A computer with a mouse is recommended.
